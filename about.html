About Castro

Making Mathematics and Computer Studies feel obvious.

Castro Odhiambo Otieno trains the next generation of Kenyan learners in Mathematics and Computer Studies, blending classroom pedagogy with practical digital-skills training.

Biography

A B.Ed Science educator, grounded in practice

Castro is completing a Bachelor of Education in Science, majoring in Mathematics & Computer Studies, at Masinde Muliro University of Science and Technology (MMUST) — Registration No. EDS/B/01-02032/2022.

Beyond the lecture hall, he has taken his pedagogy into real classrooms through teaching practice, and channels his digital-skills expertise into practical training, authorship, and tools for learners — including this very platform.

He is fluent across Microsoft Excel, MS Access, Canva, CapCut, and professional document writing, and treats digital literacy as a core life skill rather than an elective.
