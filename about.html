About Castro

Where academic rigor meets everyday digital problem-solving

I believe that mathematics, computing and public digital services all sit on the same foundation: clear thinking, patient explanation, and following a process until it actually works. That belief shapes how I teach and how I serve every client.

Biography

From a classroom in Samanga to a university lecture hall at MMUST

My path into education started early, at Samanga Lutheran Mixed Secondary School, where I completed my secondary studies in 2021. Mathematics and computing were always the subjects I gravitated toward — not because they came easily, but because they rewarded patient, structured thinking.

That pull led me to Masinde Muliro University of Science and Technology (MMUST), where I am currently a B.Ed (Science) candidate specializing in Mathematics & Computer Studies. University coursework sharpened the theory, but it was the return to real classrooms — first through long-holiday tuition at Samanga, then through a full teaching attachment at Kuoyo Kochia Boys High School in 2025 — that taught me how those subjects actually land with a teenager sitting an exam in a few months.

Alongside teaching, I've extended the same explain-it-clearly instinct into digital services: helping students and families navigate KRA, KUCCPS, HELB and e-Citizen portals that are often more confusing than the exams themselves. Two published titles, "Introduction to Digital Competency" and "Computer Studies and ICT Practical Guide," grew directly out of that classroom-to-cyber-café experience.
